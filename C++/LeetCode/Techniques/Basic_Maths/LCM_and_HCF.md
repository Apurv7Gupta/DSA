# LCM

The smallest positive integer that is a multiple of both numbers.

### Prime Factorization method

1. Prime factorize both numbers.
2. Take each prime with its highest exponent from either factorization.
3. Multiply them together.

```
12 = 2² × 3
18 = 2 × 3²

LCM = 2² × 3² = 36
```

### Cake Method

Write both numbers side by side.
Repeatedly divide by common prime factors
(left divisors) × (bottom numbers)

```
2 | 12   18
3 |  6    9
  |  2    3      ← stop (no common factor)


LCM = 2 × 3 × 2 × 3 = 36
```

# HCF (also k/a GCD)

The largest positive integer that divides both numbers exactly.

```
Factors of 12: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12
Factors of 18: 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18
```

Common factors: `1, 2, 3, 6`

So HCF = `6`.

1. Write both numbers side by side.
2. Repeatedly divide by common prime factors
3. HCF = multiply the left column only.

```
2 | 12   18
3 |  6    9
  |  2    3

HCF = 2 × 3 = 6
```
